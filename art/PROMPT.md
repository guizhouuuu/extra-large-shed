# Sprite provenance

Generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. The local vanilla Big Shed sprite was used only as a style/geometry reference. No game tilesheets or original building texture are included in this source project.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art building sprite for a Stardew Valley 1.6 mod, one transparent PNG. Create a distinct next-tier upgrade of the reference Big Shed called Extra Large Shed. The reference is a style/geometry reference; retain its crisp native pixel grid, warm timber palette, steep red shingle roof, dark brown outlines, exact front/three-quarter orthographic game view, centered red wooden door at the very bottom and overall narrow 7-tile silhouette. Make the upgraded shed recognizable through two small symmetrical upper loft windows/dormers, slightly sturdier timber framing, tidy copper roof ridge finials and a modest stone plinth, with restrained detail matching original Stardew Valley pixel art. No signs, writing, runes, magic, people, ground, shadow outside the building, or background. IMPORTANT target native sprite canvas exactly 112 pixels wide by 128 pixels tall, building fully inside that canvas, red centered door must occupy x48..63 near y104..127. Transparent corners. Do not render enlarged square pixels as a textured painting: create hard-edged true low-resolution pixel art with a limited palette and no antialiasing. Three visibly distinct material regions are required for vanilla repainting: warm wood wall body, red roof tiles, golden-brown structural trim; keep glass, stones and very dark outlines neutral. Roof cannot extend horizontally beyond the original 112-pixel footprint. Deliver only the single sprite.

The generated large source is packed deterministically into the native grid with `tools/pack-art.py`. The paint mask is technical channel data, not a second artistic render. Glass, chimney, stone and dark outlines are excluded from recoloring where classified.
